## 2024-05-23 - [Log Filtering Optimization] **Learning:** When filtering log records, checking for existence of `record.args` (boolean check) is significantly faster than comparing `record.msg` against a long template string. Optimizing the order of conditional checks in frequently called filters (like `logging.Filter`) is a valid micro-optimization. **Action:** Always order boolean/existence checks before string comparisons in tight loops or hot paths.
