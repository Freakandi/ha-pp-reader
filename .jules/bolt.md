## 2025-02-18 - [Vectorized Security Wealth Calculation]
**Learning:** `_calculate_security_wealth` in `engine_pandas.py` was iterating over every security column (O(N_Securities)) to divide by FX rates. This involved repetitive Series creation and division for each security.
**Action:** Replaced the loop with matrix operations. We now calculate native wealth for all securities at once (`Holdings * Prices`), group columns by currency, sum them (O(N_Currencies)), and perform the FX division on the aggregated currency sums. This reduced the benchmark runtime from ~1.40s to ~0.11s (~92% reduction) for 500 securities over 365 days.
